# The Portugal Guide

An independent, editorial travel-guide project made in Portugal. The goal is to help visitors make better decisions, use their time well and discover more than the obvious highlights.

## Live website

https://edicruz603-stack.github.io/the-portugal-guide/

## Product vision

Build a family of destination guides under one consistent brand and design system. Each destination should feel distinctive, but share the same practical standards:

- Clear, researched destination guidance.
- Realistic itineraries and route planning.
- Interactive maps and useful location links.
- Food, culture, free discoveries and seasonal events.
- Accessibility, transport, weather and contingency advice.
- A polished downloadable PDF companion.
- Editorially verified information with visible review dates.

The romantic editorial treatment is the creative direction for the first Sintra edition; it should not force every future destination into the same tone. Lisbon, for example, will need its own urban identity and content structure while remaining recognisably part of The Portugal Guide.

## Product and launch strategy

- First destination: **Sintra**, including the historic centre, mountain, Colares and the Cabo da Roca/coastal area.
- Initial language: **English (UK)**.
- Audience: international visitors, including first-time travellers, couples, families, solo travellers and returning visitors.
- Proposed launch price: **€9.50** for the first edition, as an introductory price to validate demand and gather feedback.
- The launch price is a commercial decision, not a live offer. Do not publish a checkout or collect payments until the guide is complete and legal, tax, consumer, privacy and invoicing requirements have been checked.
- No paid services, subscriptions, domains, stock assets or other expenses without explicit approval.
- Start with a one-off purchase. Consider recurring paid updates only after there is a real update programme and evidence of customer demand.

## Destination roadmap

1. **Sintra** — first complete product and quality benchmark.
2. **Lisbon** — next major guide, with neighbourhood-based exploration, transport, viewpoints, food, history, museums, day planning and practical city navigation.
3. **Further Portugal destinations** — prioritised after Sintra and Lisbon based on visitor demand, research quality and the ability to provide genuinely useful local insight.

Do not treat future destinations as simple copies of Sintra. Reuse the technical components and editorial standards, but research each place independently and give it its own identity.

## Repository structure

- `index.html` — brand homepage and destination directory.
- `sintra.html` — core Sintra field guide and interactive day planner.
- `sintra-local-discoveries.html` — free discoveries, food, museums and seasonal diary.
- `sintra-romantic-guide.html` — romantic editorial companion and suggested itineraries.
- `styles.css` — shared responsive editorial styling.
- `script.js` — shared mobile navigation and interactive planner logic.

## Before launch

1. Audit every page, navigation link, map marker, external link and interactive feature.
2. Verify key facts, ticket prices, opening hours, transport, accessibility and event dates against authoritative sources.
3. Review all images, credits and licences for the intended commercial use.
4. Complete the mobile and desktop experience, including keyboard and accessibility checks.
5. Proofread the downloadable PDF against the live guide and host the approved final PDF in the public product flow.
6. Add the necessary business identity, contact, privacy, terms, consumer and refund information.
7. Confirm Portuguese/EU tax, VAT, invoicing and digital-content obligations with an appropriately qualified source.
8. Compare payment providers by fees, payout availability and compliance requirements before selecting one.
