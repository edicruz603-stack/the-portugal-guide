WHEN TO GO

BOOKING: WHAT THE OFFICIAL PAGES SAY

Spring and autumn often give a comfortable balance of
temperature and daylight; summer brings longer days and
heavier demand; winter can be atmospheric but foggy,
wet and windy. The hills are usually damper and cooler
than the coast, so a light waterproof is sensible in any
season.
This is editorial advice, not a climate claim.

Pena: the time on your ticket is your entry time to the palace, not to
the park. The palace is at the top of a hill inside the park; allow
about 30 minutes from the park entrance.
Ticket offices at the Moorish Castle, the Capuchos, Monserrate and
the National Palace close from 12:00 to 13:00; automatic machines
are available.
Regaleira is a separate operator with timed slots, sold only through
its official online ticket office. Do not assume a combined ticket.
Scotturb 24-hour pass for buses 434 and 435: €10.96 through the
Parques de Sintra ticket office. Transport only.

HOW MUCH TO ATTEMPT

Half a day: the historic centre and one nearby monument. Do not
add the hills.
One day: one hill pairing (the Moorish Castle and Pena) or one
western pairing (Regaleira and Monserrate), with the old town if
energy allows.
Two days: the town and the western side on one day, the castle and
Pena on the other, or the coast as an alternative.
WHAT TO BRING

Shoes with grip, water, a light waterproof, sun protection,
a charged phone and your tickets. Expect cobbles, uneven
paths, slopes and steps.

Book the Pena palace slot first. The time on the ticket is
your entry time to the palace, which is at the top of a
hill inside the park; allow about 30 minutes from the
park entrance.
Take bus 434 from the station: it passes the National
Palace and serves the Castle and Pena.
The Castle opens at 09:30 and takes its last admission at
17:30; its ticket office closes from 12:00 to 13:00.
The Park of Pena stays open until 19:00 (last entry
18:00): the best hour for the park is often the one after
the palace.
A full day. Do not add Monserrate or the coast without your

Regaleira before lunch and Monserrate after is a comfortable
order.
